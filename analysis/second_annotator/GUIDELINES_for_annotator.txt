Labelling guide: second-annotator check of generated Urdu data

Thank you for helping. The file sheet.xlsx has 330 short items in Urdu (Nastaliq) and
Roman Urdu. Each one was written by an AI system (Grok) and given a label. We want to
know whether an independent native speaker agrees with those labels. You will not see
the original labels. That is deliberate, so please don't ask about them until you finish.

Content warning: some items are hateful, abusive or insulting, including about religion,
ethnicity and public figures. You can take breaks or stop at any time.

What to fill in (three columns; the first two have drop-down lists):

1. annotator_label: the label you think is correct, chosen from allowed_labels.
2. natural: "yes" if a real person could plausibly have written this text (on social
   media, in a review, in a quiz and so on), "no" if it reads as artificial or machine-like.
3. comment: optional. Use it when an item is unclear, could fit two labels, or has a
   language problem.

Work alone. Don't discuss items with anyone, including me, until the sheet is finished.
If an item is unclear, choose the label that fits best and explain in the comment. Please
don't leave any item without a label.

What the labels mean

Sentiment (Negative / Positive)
  Does the text express a negative or a positive view?

3-class polarity (Negative / Neutral / Positive)
  Neutral means no clear opinion: a fact, a question, or a mixed or balanced remark.

Hate speech (Normal / Hate)
  Hate: the text attacks, insults, demeans or threatens a person or group, or uses slurs.
  Criticism, complaint or disagreement on its own, without an attack, is Normal.

Cyber abuse (Normal / Abusive)
  Abusive: the text insults, harasses, swears at or threatens someone (for example a
  content creator or another commenter). Rude but non-abusive feedback is Normal.

QA pair validation (Invalid / Valid)
  Each item has a question and an answer. Valid: the answer actually answers this
  question (even if briefly). Invalid: the answer belongs to a different question.

When you finish
  Save the file as sheet.xlsx (keep the Excel format, don't convert it to CSV) and send it back.
  It should take about 2 to 3 hours. You can do it in several sittings.

Your name will appear in the paper as the second annotator and in the acknowledgments.
Tell me if you would rather not be named.
