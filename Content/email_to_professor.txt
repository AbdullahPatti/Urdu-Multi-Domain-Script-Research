  everyday social text, the other is narrowly focused on political and governance themes.

Roman Urdu Sentiment Analysis — Domain A (Social Media) platform clarification:

  "Dataset 11000 Reviews.tsv" covers multiple content types collected from social media,
  including political commentary, product opinions, and drama/entertainment discussions.
  It is not platform-specific; it aggregates text from various online social sources.
  This domain is labeled "Social Media (Politics, Products, Drama)" to reflect its
  mixed-source nature. The other domains are clearly distinct: YouTube comments focused
  on entertainment, and a separate corpus covering general social topics such as
  utilities, apps, and courses.

Nastaliq Hate Speech — difference between Offensive and Hate Speech datasets:

  These are two independently published datasets collected separately by different
  research groups with different annotation guidelines and different source corpora.
  "22K_Offensive_dataset_Final.xlsx" focuses on offensiveness in general online social
  media text. "Urdu_Hate_Speech.xlsx" is a separately collected corpus specifically
  targeting hate speech with a different annotation scheme. Beyond these two, we have
  two additional domain datasets: one covering politics, sports, health, and family
  culture, and another covering sports, labor, arts, and education — both representing
  clearly different topical domains.

Nastaliq Fake News — clarification of domain differences:

  The four datasets come from distinctly different sources and contexts:
    — Multi-Topic News Corpus: structured news articles across business, health, sports,
      and technology categories, collected from news sources.
    — Ax-to-Grind Dataset: independently published in its own research paper, focused
      on agenda-driven fake news — a completely separate collection effort.
    — General Pakistani News: a merged dataset of fake and real news articles from
      broad Pakistani online news sources (two halves of one dataset combined).
    — Notri-Fact Platform: news items verified or refuted by a dedicated fact-checking
      platform — a fundamentally different source type from news publishers.
  The domains differ in source type, collection methodology, and topical focus.
